# <ref item="minecraft:bamboo"/>

There are no trees on this sky island—only <ref item="minecraft:bamboo"/>. You will need it to take your first steps toward progress.

# <ref item="skyland:bamboo_leaves"/>

<item id="skyland:bamboo_leaves" />

- Naturally generated <ref item="minecraft:bamboo"/> has a chance to grow leaves. Break the leaves to obtain <ref item="skyland:bamboo_leaves"/>.
- <ref item="skyland:bamboo_leaves"/> has no other use and can only be composted in a <ref item="minecraft:composter"/>.

# <ref item="minecraft:crafting_table"/>

- Use the recipes below to craft a <ref item="minecraft:crafting_table"/> from <ref item="minecraft:bamboo"/>.
- Arrange the ingredients carefully to avoid crafting <ref item="minecraft:bamboo_mosaic"/> by mistake.
- Once you have a crafting table, making blocks through bamboo slabs is expensive; switch to a more efficient crafting method as soon as possible.
