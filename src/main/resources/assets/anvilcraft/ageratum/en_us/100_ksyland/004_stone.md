# <ref item="skyland:pebble"/>

- Craft an <ref item="minecraft:iron_hoe"/> and till dirt for a chance to obtain <ref item="skyland:pebble"/>.
- Only <ref item="minecraft:iron_hoe"/> and <ref item="minecraft:stone_hoe"/> can obtain <ref item="skyland:pebble"/>.

# <ref item="minecraft:cobblestone"/>

Repeatedly till dirt into farmland, then trample it back into dirt to eventually obtain <ref item="minecraft:cobblestone"/>.
