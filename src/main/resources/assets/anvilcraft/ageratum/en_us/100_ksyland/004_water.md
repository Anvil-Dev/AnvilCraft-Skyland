# <ref item="minecraft:cauldron"/>

- After crafting a <ref item="minecraft:cauldron"/>, rain will slowly fill it with water.
- Craft a <ref item="minecraft:bucket"/> to scoop the water out.
- Craft <ref item="minecraft:glass_bottle"/> and use water bottles to fill a cauldron. Once you have two buckets of water, you can make an infinite water source.

# <ref item="anvilcraft:crab_claw"/>

Craft an <ref item="anvilcraft:crab_trap"/> to obtain <ref item="anvilcraft:crab_claw"/>.

# <ref item="minecraft:snow_block"/>

- In a few environments, it snows instead of raining.
- Collect <ref item="minecraft:snowball"/>, craft <ref item="minecraft:ice"/>, and melt it into water.
