# <ref item="skyland:pebble"/>

- Place <ref item="minecraft:cobblestone"/> in a *humid biome*, keep its light level at 0, and ensure there is air above it.
- The <ref item="minecraft:cobblestone"/> will slowly turn into <ref item="minecraft:mossy_cobblestone"/>.
- Use an axe to scrape off <ref item="skyland:moss"/>.

# <ref item="minecraft:azalea"/>

- Use <ref item="minecraft:bone_meal"/> on <ref item="minecraft:moss_block"/> for a chance to grow an <ref item="minecraft:azalea"/>.
- Use <ref item="minecraft:bone_meal"/> on an <ref item="minecraft:azalea"/> for a chance to grow a tree.
- You can finally obtain wood in large quantities.

# More <ref item="minecraft:dirt"/>

- First obtain <ref item="minecraft:stone"/>, either by smelting or by letting lava touch a water source block.
- Use <ref item="minecraft:bone_meal"/> on <ref item="minecraft:moss_block"/> to convert nearby <ref item="minecraft:stone"/>.
- Plant an <ref item="minecraft:azalea"/> on <ref item="minecraft:moss_block"/> to generate <ref item="minecraft:rooted_dirt"/>.
- Till <ref item="minecraft:rooted_dirt"/> with a hoe to obtain <ref item="minecraft:dirt"/>.
- Compared with consuming <ref item="minecraft:dirt"/> directly, this method saves many <ref item="skyland:stone_anvil"/>s.
