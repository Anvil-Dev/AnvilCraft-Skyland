# Resume the AnvilCraft Progression

- For the next steps, follow the “AnvilCraft Manual / Main Progression.”
- Most items have uncommon acquisition methods in vanilla Minecraft.
- This chapter covers only the rare items added by this addon or by AnvilCraft itself, along with their acquisition methods.
- A few important vanilla acquisition methods are also noted here.

# <ref item="minecraft:quartz"/>

There are two ways to obtain it:

1. Barter with piglins for <ref item="minecraft:quartz"/>.
2. Trade with a Mason for <ref item="minecraft:quartz_block"/>, then unpack it into <ref item="minecraft:quartz"/>.

# <ref item="anvilcraft:sponge_gemmule"/>

Fishing has a 0.0001% chance to yield <ref item="anvilcraft:sponge_gemmule"/>.

# <ref item="minecraft:nether_star"/>

- Travel to the Nether, find a *Nether structure*, and build a mob farm to obtain <ref item="minecraft:wither_skeleton_skull"/>.
- Trade with piglins in the Nether to obtain <ref item="minecraft:soul_sand"/>.
